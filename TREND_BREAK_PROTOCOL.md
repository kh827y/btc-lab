# TREND-002 — broken-trend recovery, descriptive feasibility before outcomes

Decision D1/D3. Separate from TREND-001, which compared levels after a first 20% drawdown. This is a single fixed weekly state transition, not a claim that MA50 identifies true bull/bear regimes. No tuning, new forecast, confirmation authorization, or 2024+ numeric values. Primary comparison for later descriptive scoring: wait for recovery versus fixed one-week delay; immediate and half-now/half-in-one-week are additional controls. Equal $1 budget, fixed 0.1% entry fee per allocated dollar, no cash yield, leverage, exit fee, slippage or existing portfolio. Monday UTC open proxy; signals use Saturday UTC close and trailing 50 valid daily closes including Saturday, prior weekly state from preceding Saturday. Sunday gap allows calculation. State above if close strictly above MA50; at/below otherwise. Need contiguous 50 positive-volume candles within declared start; no missing/zero-volume substitution.

Episode begins at first Monday whose known state is at/below after a prior valid above state. Ends at first subsequent valid above state; first missing weekly state censors active episode and resets previous state, with no inferred transition across gap. Initial at/below without preceding observed above does not start an episode; count left-unobserved stretches and invalid state dates. End-exclusive 2024-01-01 censors open episodes. Include all triggers irrespective of subsequent recovery. Never select based on trough, profitability, or later episode shape.

Four schedules: immediate full at origin; full at origin+7 days; half each; full at first subsequent recovery Monday strictly before common evaluation terminal; otherwise cash through terminal if no observed recovery, or unknown if censored before terminal. For horizons 28 (primary), 90 (secondary), common terminal origin+h at UTC open. The same recovery rule is observed through both dates: no entry at terminal or later counted in that horizon. A complete comparison requires all positive-volume days origin through terminal inclusive, and known recovery action through terminal (a gap at terminal is irrelevant to the signal only if all needed price days valid). No fill, no gap repair. Horizon eligibility does not depend on direction/return. Current date never used.

Primary common-history venues: pinned BUY Bitstamp/Coinbase, 2015-07-20 through 2023-12-31, matching calendar/warmup; use same fixed definitions separately. Early Bitstamp sensitivity: exact pinned overlap with early CSV, history starting 2012-01-01; not an independent market or held-out test. Do not silently combine venues. First run reports only signal dates, episode status, coverage eligibility, waiting/schedule categories, count differences vs fixed7, calendar-year counts and overlapping-window clusters for each horizon. No price levels, entry prices, returns, P&L, downside or outcome comparisons in preflight. Reading past closes to establish signal times and existence of bars is necessary, not outcome scoring. Clusters are connected components of inclusive origin-to-terminal windows, not an effective sample size. Cross-venue exact trigger date counts only, not independent samples.

Preflight reviewed before adding any outcome scoring code. If no meaningful schedule variation beyond a fixed one-week delay, or differences confined to one overlap cluster, stop with descriptive feasibility report; otherwise assess dispersion across periods and explicitly decide whether descriptive comparison is worth doing. No statistical power threshold implied; no p-values or confirmed investment skill. If continued, compare paired terminal wealth including cash and BTC quantity, downside from initial budget (daily low and terminal open), and drawdown from intermediate daily closing/open marks separately; report closing-path downside sensitivity due known Coinbase extreme, all exclusions and dependence. Never delete or repair original inputs. No tuning of MA length or crossing rules. Store immutable reports, separate from frozen BUY/RISK and monitoring.
