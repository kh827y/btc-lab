One first close at least 20% below prior observed closing high per peak-to-recovery episode from DD-001 peak_history. No selection by eventual depth or recovered status; uncertain peak and insufficient common 200-day MA history excluded with reason. Include right-censored episodes in inventory. Complete comparison requires positive-volume uninterrupted bars from immediate entry through common terminal day. No post-2023 numeric prices.

Three fixed illustrative policies: immediate purchase at next day's open after threshold close; wait for first close strictly above trailing MA50 or MA200 (includes that close), starting at threshold day, buy next day's open. If already above, immediate entry and flag; these are level tests not crossing or proven trend reversal. Common terminal = immediate entry + 90 calendar days, no purchase at terminal, cash if no eligible signal before it. No stopping at hindsight trough or peak recovery. Equal $1 starting cash, .1% entry fee, no cash yield, taxes/slippage/exit cost or additional purchases. All three on common eligible episodes, gaps explicitly censored, no replacing missing observations. Show per-episode dates and price, waiting days, entry premium vs immediate and vs lowest daily low known from episode start through signal close; no hindsight bottom required. Median outcomes and episode counts, no p-values, no independent-cycle claims, cross-check exact trigger dates on both venues (do not force matches).
