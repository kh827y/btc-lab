## Decisions, not generic factor scores

Owner objective already stated: position investing weeks–months, buy confirmed trend, buy dips while trend persists, consider deep value drawdowns before a reversal if risk/reward is attractive. Sell decisions remain with owner. No auto-trading. The owner has NOT supplied a fixed portfolio drawdown tolerance, minimum useful gain, cash reserve target, or risk utility. Do not infer these from prior positions or use arbitrary thresholds as owner preferences.

- D1 — Buy now, defer a planned purchase, or split it: 7/28-day decision. Compare equal available budget and identical terminal date, explicit cash including yield/cost assumptions, quantity acquired, terminal wealth including unspent cash, downside and regret versus immediate purchase and DCA. Record time actually delayed and exposure. Never claim a good forecast automatically implies a good purchase.
- D2 — How much of available cash to deploy during a deep decline: 28/90-day decision. Report distributions of further decline from entry, endpoint return, time to recovery with censoring, and terminal wealth/portfolio drawdown across fixed illustrative initial deployment fractions. No optimal fraction or universal drawdown cap until owner chooses tolerances. BTC quantity alone is not the objective when capital stays in cash.
- D3 — Assess whether trend recovery or downside regime change is credible: 7/28/90-day distributions and event context. Distinguish direction, volatility, downside and valuation; report base rates and comparable historical episodes, uncertainty and conflicting evidence. No deterministic buy/sell trigger.

These are working research objectives derived from owner goals, not an authorized investment policy. Illustrative capital allocations and regret/risk tradeoffs must be labeled, never personalized advice from invented tolerance.

## Gates for new work

Initial FLOW/VALUE/FUND/OI/MACRO/COMB screening closed. Stop repeated single-factor template, M2 tuning, eight-week delay rules and the tested CPI buy rule. Keep past negative results and preserve all source data. RESEARCH_REGISTRY.json is a retrospective inventory of eight closed study families, not a claim of prior registration or exhaustive audit of every scalar result. It enumerates named forecast pairs and comparison/sensitivity families; all original code, protocols and local reports remain authoritative. Future registration must enumerate every planned primary/secondary comparison explicitly before results and append all attempts. Do not silently overwrite old entries. No new confirmatory study until target, decision, comparator, horizon, useful effect, data vintage, evaluation dates, multiplicity/dependence and stopping rule registered. Default one primary comparison per study and at most three new confirmatory studies per calendar quarter; only change before results with owner approval.

Mandatory synthetic gate for forecast or decision confirmation: define null worlds and planted effect of practically meaningful size before running; include dependence/regime shifts, relevant drift/volatility and actual decision cadence; compare against strong/simple baseline and oracle positive control. Measure false leads and power across fixed seeds, uncertainty of estimates, and decision leverage with realistically useful effects. Provisional diagnostic criteria for each protocol to justify explicitly: target >=80% detection and <=10% false positive rate (with Monte Carlo uncertainty; not claims about real BTC). If not met, report insufficient design/power, redesign before real outcomes or mark descriptive-only. No tuning synthetic worlds to make results pass. Descriptive base-rate tables need validity and sample/episode checks, not an artificial skill detection gate. No actual gate implementation or replication of Fable's synthetic results yet.
