# DELEVER-001 — post-shock market context (descriptive)
Frozen before selecting episodes or reading forward outcomes. Separate from historical OI-001, OI-GATE-001 and ABSORB-001; no forecast, causal or trading-rule authorization. Decision D1/D3: following a large price decline, does a substantial contraction in perpetual open interest accompany a different cost/risk of waiting seven days? A reduction in open positions is not proof of forced liquidations, reduced leverage per trader, or persistent spot demand.

- Reviewed Binance BTCUSDT spot and USD-M perpetual hourly panel: 2020-09-01 through 2023-12-31 UTC inclusive. Calendar eligibility for 7d outcome only if entry plus 168 hours lies strictly before 2024. Preflight does not read any outcome prices or decide eligibility using them.
## Evaluation (not yet authorized)
Primary: difference in median simple 7-day wait cost (price at entry+168h / entry price - 1), contraction group minus other group, alongside both raw distributions and all eligible price-only episodes. Positive wait cost means later entry is more expensive; compare equal budget, same assumed proportional fee, no cash yield (relative BTC quantity for delayed vs immediate is 1/(1+cost), split half now/half later). No claim of profit or optimal allocation. Secondary: means, proportion cost positive, worst entry-relative spot low during the same 168 hours including terminal open, and 28-day wait cost only when calendar eligible (overlap explicitly reported). Missing endpoints exclude only the affected outcome; no filling or fallback; risk requires all intervening spot lows. Report missingness by group. Show group comparison separately by year and drop bands as descriptive support/limitations, no significance tests, no fitted model, no independent-observation claim. 2020–23 price outcomes already exposed, not holdout. Preflight assesses counts and overlap before separate approval; if fewer than ten in either group or no common price-drop band containing at least five in each, close as case inventory only (practical reporting floor, not power). Even adequate counts cannot establish a causal effect. No alternative thresholds or repeated outcome tests. Record prior exposure, preflight and outcome hashes in registry; preserve all reports.
